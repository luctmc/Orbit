# Code of conduct

Everyone participating in Orbit's open-source community is expected to interact respectfully and constructively, regardless of experience or background.

Please be considerate, give actionable technical feedback, assume good faith when appropriate, and respect other contributors' privacy and intellectual property. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, and publishing another person's sensitive information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove abusive material, moderate discussions and restrict participation when necessary. To report inappropriate conduct, contact the maintainers privately through their GitHub profiles. Do not post personal or sensitive reports in public issues.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews and other project-managed community spaces.
